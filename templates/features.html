System Features

Lane Detection

The system continuously analyzes road markings and ensures that the vehicle stays within its lane. It intelligently detects curves, broken lines, and faded markings, helping drivers maintain safe positioning even in complex road conditions.

Pothole Detection

Using advanced computer vision, the system identifies damaged road surfaces such as potholes and cracks. It alerts drivers in real-time, reducing the risk of accidents and improving overall driving safety.

Collision Warning

The system predicts potential collisions by analyzing nearby vehicles and obstacles. It provides early warnings, giving drivers enough time to react and avoid accidents.

GPS Tracking

Real-time GPS tracking allows continuous monitoring of vehicle location. It enhances navigation, route optimization, and supports smart transportation systems.

Smart Alerts & Notifications

The system delivers instant alerts and notifications based on road conditions. Instead of complex data, it provides simple and clear warnings that drivers can easily understand.

Warning System

Advanced warning mechanisms combine visual and audio alerts to notify drivers of potential dangers. This ensures quick response and enhances road safety significantly.
